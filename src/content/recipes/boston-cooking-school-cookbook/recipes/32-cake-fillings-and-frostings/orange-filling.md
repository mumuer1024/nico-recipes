# Orange Filling

## Ingredients

- ½ cup sugar
- 2½ tablespoons flour
- Grated rind ½ orange
- ¼ cup orange juice
- ½ tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 egg slightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon butter

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given. Cook ten minutes in double boiler, stirring constantly. Cool before spreading.

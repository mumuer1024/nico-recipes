# Caramel Frosting II

## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups sugar
- ⅔ cup grated maple sugar
- ½ cup butter
- ⅔ cup cream

## Method

Mix ingredients and boil thirteen minutes. Beat until of right consistency to spread.

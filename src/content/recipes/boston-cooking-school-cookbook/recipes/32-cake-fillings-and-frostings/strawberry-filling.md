# Strawberry Filling

## Ingredients

- 1 cup thick cream
- ⅓ cup sugar
- White 1 egg
- ½ cup strawberries
- ½ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Beat cream until stiff, using Dover egg-beater, add sugar, white of egg beaten until stiff, strawberries mashed, and vanilla.

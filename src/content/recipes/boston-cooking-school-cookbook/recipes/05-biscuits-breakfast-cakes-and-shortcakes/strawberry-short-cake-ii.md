# Strawberry Short Cake II

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- ⅓ cup butter
- ¾ cup milk

## Method

Mix same as Strawberry Short Cake I. Toss and roll on floured board. Put in round buttered tin, and shape with back of hand to fit pan.

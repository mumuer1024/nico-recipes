# One Egg Muffins II

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 egg

## Method

Mix and bake as One Egg Muffin I.

# Queen of Muffins

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ cup milk (scant)
- 1½ cups flour
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder

## Method

Mix and bake same as Twin Mountain Muffins.

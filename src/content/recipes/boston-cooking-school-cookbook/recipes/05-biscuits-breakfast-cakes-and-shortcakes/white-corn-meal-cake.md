# White Corn Meal Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 cup scalded milk
- ½ cup white corn meal
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Method

Add salt to corn meal, and pour on gradually milk. Turn into a buttered shallow pan to the depth of one-fourth inch. Bake in a moderate oven until crisp. Split and spread with butter.

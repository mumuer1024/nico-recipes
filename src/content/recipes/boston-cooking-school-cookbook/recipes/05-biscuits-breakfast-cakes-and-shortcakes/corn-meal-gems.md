# Corn Meal Gems

## Ingredients

- ½ cup corn meal
- 1 cup flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon melted butter
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¾ cup milk
- 1 egg

## Method

Mix and bake as Graham Muffins II.

# Oatmeal Muffins

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked oatmeal
- 1½ cups flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Method

Mix and bake as Rice Muffins.

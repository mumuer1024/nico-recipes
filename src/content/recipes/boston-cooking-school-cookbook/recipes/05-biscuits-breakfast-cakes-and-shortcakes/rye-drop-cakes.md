# Rye Drop Cakes

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup rye meal
- ⅔ cup flour
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons molasses
- ½ cup milk
- 1 egg

## Method

Mix and sift dry ingredients; add milk gradually, molasses, and egg well beaten. Cook same as Fried Drop Cakes.

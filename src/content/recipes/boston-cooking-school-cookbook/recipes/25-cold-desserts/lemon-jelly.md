# Lemon Jelly

## Ingredients

- ½ box gelatine or
- 2 tablespoons granulated gelatine
- ½ cup cold water
- 2½ cups boiling water
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup lemon juice

## Method

Soak gelatine twenty minutes in cold water, dissolve in boiling water, strain, and add to sugar and lemon juice. Turn into mould, and chill.

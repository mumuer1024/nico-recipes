# Coffee Custard

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons ground coffee
- 3 eggs
- ¼ cup sugar
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Scald milk with coffee, and strain. Beat eggs slightly; add sugar, salt, vanilla, and milk. Strain into buttered individual moulds, set in pan of hot water, and bake until firm.

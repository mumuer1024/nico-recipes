# Strawberry Sponge

## Ingredients

- ⅓ box gelatine or
- 1⅓ tablespoons granulated gelatine
- ⅓ cup cold water
- ⅓ cup boiling water
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 cup strawberry juice
- Whites 3 eggs
- Whip from 3 cups cream

## Method

Make same as Orange Charlotte.

# Fruit Salad I

## Method

Arrange alternate layers of shredded pineapple, sliced bananas, and sliced oranges, sprinkling each layer with powdered sugar. Chill before serving.

*To Shred Pineapple.* Pare and cut out eyes, pick off small pieces with
a silver fork, continuing until all soft part is removed. *To Slice
Oranges.* Remove skin and white covering, slice lengthwise that the
tough centre may not be served; seeds should be removed.

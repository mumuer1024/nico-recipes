# Orange Puffs

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 1¾ cups flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder

## Method

Mix same as Cottage Pudding, and bake in buttered individual tins. Serve with Orange Sauce.

# Cream Sauce

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla
- ¼ cup heavy cream

## Method

Cream the butter, add sugar gradually, vanilla, and cream beaten until stiff.

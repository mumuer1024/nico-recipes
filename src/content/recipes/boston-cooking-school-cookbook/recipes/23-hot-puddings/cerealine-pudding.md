# Cerealine Pudding

## Ingredients

- 4 cups scalded milk
- 2 cups cerealine
- ½ cup molasses
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- 1½ tablespoons butter

## Method

Pour milk on cerealine, add remaining ingredients, pour into buttered pudding-dish, and bake one hour in slow oven. Serve with cream.

# Harvard Pudding

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- ½ cup sugar
- 2½ cups flour
- 3½ teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk

## Method

Mix and sift dry ingredients and work in butter with tips of fingers; beat egg, add milk, and combine mixtures; turn into buttered mould, cover, and steam two hours; serve with warm Apple Sauce and Hard Sauce.

**Apple Sauce.** Pick over and wash dried apples, soak over night in
cold water to cover; cook until soft; sweeten, and flavor with lemon
juice.

# German Punch

## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 1¾ cups tomatoes
- 3 apples, cored, pared, and chopped
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- Piece ginger root
- 3 tablespoons Maraschino

## Method

Mix ingredients, except cordial, and cook thirty-five minutes. Rub through a sieve, add Maraschino, and freeze to a mush.

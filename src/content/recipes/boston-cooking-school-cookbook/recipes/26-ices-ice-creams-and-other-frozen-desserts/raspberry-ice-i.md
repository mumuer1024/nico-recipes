# Raspberry Ice I

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 1⅔ cups sugar
- 2 cups raspberry juice
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Method

Make a syrup as for Lemon Ice, cool, add raspberries mashed, and squeezed through double cheese-cloth, and lemon juice; strain and freeze.

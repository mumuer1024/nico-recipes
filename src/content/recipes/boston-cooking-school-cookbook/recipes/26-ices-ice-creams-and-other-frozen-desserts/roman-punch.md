# Roman Punch

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup lemon juice
- ½ cup orange juice
- ½ cup tea infusion
- ½ cup rum

## Method

Prepare and freeze same as Cardinal Punch.

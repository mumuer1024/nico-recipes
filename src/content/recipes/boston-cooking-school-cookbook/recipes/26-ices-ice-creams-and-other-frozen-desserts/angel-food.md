# Angel Food

## Ingredients

- Whites 3 eggs
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 1 quart cream whip
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla

## Method

Beat eggs until stiff, fold in sugar, cream whip, and flavoring; line a mould with Delmonico Ice Cream, fill with the mixture, cover, pack in salt and ice, and let stand two hours.

# Orange Ice Cream

## Ingredients

- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup thin cream
- 2 cups orange juice
- Sugar

## Method

Add cream slowly to orange juice, sweeten to taste, and freeze. Serve with canned strawberries or fresh fruit mashed and sweetened.

# Italian Sorbet

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- 1½ cups orange juice
- 1½ cups grape fruit juice
- ½ cup lemon juice
- ¼ cup wine

## Method

Prepare and freeze same as Pineapple Frappé.

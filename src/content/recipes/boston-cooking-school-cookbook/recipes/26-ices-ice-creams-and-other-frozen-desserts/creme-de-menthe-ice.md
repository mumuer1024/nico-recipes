# Crême de Menthe Ice

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup Crême de Menthe cordial
- Burnett's Leaf Green

## Method

Make a syrup as for Lemon Ice, add cordial and coloring; strain and freeze.

# Chocolate Sauce II

## Ingredients

- 1 square Baker's chocolate
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon butter
- ⅓ cup water
- ½ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Melt chocolate; add butter, sugar, and water. Let boil fifteen minutes, cool slightly, and add vanilla.

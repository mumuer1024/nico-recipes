# Lemon Ice

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- ¾ cup lemon juice

## Method

Make a syrup by boiling water and sugar twenty minutes; add lemon juice; cool, strain, and freeze. See directions for freezing, page 434.

# Victoria Punch

## Ingredients

- 3½ cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup lemon juice
- ½ cup orange juice
- Grated rind two oranges
- 1 cup angelica wine
- 1 cup cider
- 1½ tablespoons gin

## Method

Prepare same as Cardinal Punch; strain before freezing, to remove orange rind.

# Pomona Frappé

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups sugar
- 4 cups water
- 1 quart sweet cider
- 2 cups orange juice
- ½ cup lemon juice

## Method

Make a syrup by boiling sugar and water twenty minutes. Add cider, orange juice, and lemon juice. Cool, strain, and freeze to a mush.

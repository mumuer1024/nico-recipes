# Macaroon Ice Cream

## Ingredients

- 1 quart cream
- 1 cup macaroons
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla

## Method

Dry, pound, and measure macaroons; add to cream, sugar, and vanilla, then freeze.

# Orange Ice

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups orange juice
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- Grated rind of two oranges

## Method

Make syrup as for Lemon Ice; add fruit juice and grated rind; cool, strain, and freeze.

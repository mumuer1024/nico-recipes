# Oyster Cocktail II

## Ingredients

- 6 small raw oysters
- Tabasco Sauce
- Lemon juice
- Salt
- Grape fruit

## Method

Cut grape fruit in halves crosswise, remove tough portions, and add oysters seasoned with Tabasco, lemon juice, and salt.

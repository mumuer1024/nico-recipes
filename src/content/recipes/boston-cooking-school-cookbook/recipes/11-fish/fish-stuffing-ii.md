# Fish Stuffing II

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cracker crumbs
- ¼ cup melted butter
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- Few drops onion juice
- Parsley, Capers, Pickles: 1 teaspoon each, finely chopped

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given. This makes a dry, crumbly stuffing.

# Fish Stuffing I

## Ingredients

- ½ cup cracker crumbs
- ½ cup stale bread crumbs
- ¼ cup melted butter
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- Few drops onion juice
- ¼ cup hot water

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given.

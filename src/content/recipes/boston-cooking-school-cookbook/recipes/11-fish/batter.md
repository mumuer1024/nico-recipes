# Batter

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup bread flour
- ¾ cup milk

## Method

Beat eggs until light, add salt and pepper. Add milk slowly to flour, stir until smooth and well mixed. Combine mixtures.

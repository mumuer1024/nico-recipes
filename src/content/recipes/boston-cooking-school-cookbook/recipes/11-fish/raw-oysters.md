# Raw Oysters

## Method

Raw oysters are served on oyster plates, or in a block of ice. Place block of ice on a folded napkin on platter, and garnish the base with parsley and quarters of lemon, or ferns and lemon.

**To Block Ice for Oysters.** Use a rectangular piece of clear ice, and
with hot flatirons melt a cavity large enough to hold the oysters. Pour
water from cavity as rapidly as it forms.

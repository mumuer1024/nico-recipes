# Soft-shell Crabs

## Method

Clean crabs, sprinkle with salt and pepper, dip in crumbs, egg, and crumbs, fry in deep fat, and drain. Being light, they will rise to top of fat, and should be turned while frying. Soft-shell crabs are usually fried. Serve with Sauce Tartare.

**To Clean a Crab.** Lift and fold back the tapering points which are
found on each side of the back shell, and remove spongy substance that
lies under them. Turn crab on its
back, and with a pointed knife remove
the small piece at lower part of shell, which terminates in a point;
this is called the apron.

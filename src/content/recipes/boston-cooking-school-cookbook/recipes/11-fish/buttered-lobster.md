# Buttered Lobster

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. lobster
- 3 tablespoons butter
- Salt and pepper
- Lemon juice

## Method

Remove lobster meat from shell and chop slightly. Melt butter, add lobster, and when heated, season and serve garnished with lobster claws.

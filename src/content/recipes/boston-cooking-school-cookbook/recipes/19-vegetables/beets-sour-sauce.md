# Beets, Sour Sauce

## Method

Wash beets, and cook in boiling salted water until soft. Drain, and reserve one-half cup water in which beets were cooked. Plunge into cold water, rub off skins and cut into cubes. Reheat in

**Sour Sauce.** Melt two tablespoons butter, add two tablespoons flour,
and pour on the beet water. Add one-fourth cup, each, vinegar and cream,
one teaspoon sugar, one-half teaspoon salt, and a few grains pepper.

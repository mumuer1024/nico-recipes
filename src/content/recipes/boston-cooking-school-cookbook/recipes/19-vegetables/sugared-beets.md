# Sugared Beets

## Ingredients

- 4 hot boiled beets
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1½ tablespoons sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Method

Cut beets in one-fourth inch slices, add butter, sugar, and salt; reheat for serving.

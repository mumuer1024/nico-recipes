# Creamy Sauce I

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 2 tablespoons wine

## Method

Cream the butter, add sugar gradually, and milk and wine drop by drop. If liquids are added too fast the sauce will have a curdled appearance.

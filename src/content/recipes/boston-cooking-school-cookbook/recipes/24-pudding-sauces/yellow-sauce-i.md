# Yellow Sauce I

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla or
- ½ teaspoon vanilla and
- 1 teaspoon brandy

## Method

Beat eggs until very light, add sugar gradually and continue beating; then flavor.

# Foamy Sauce II

## Ingredients

- Whites 2 eggs
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- ¼ cup hot milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Beat eggs until stiff, add sugar gradually, and continue beating; add milk and vanilla.

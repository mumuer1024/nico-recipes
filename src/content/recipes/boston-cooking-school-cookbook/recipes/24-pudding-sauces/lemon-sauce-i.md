# Lemon Sauce I

## Ingredients

- ¾ cups sugar
- ¼ cup water
- 2 teaspoons butter
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

## Method

Make a syrup by boiling sugar and water eight minutes; remove from fire; add butter and lemon juice.

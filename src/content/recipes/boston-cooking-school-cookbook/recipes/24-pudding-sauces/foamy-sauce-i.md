# Foamy Sauce I

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons wine

## Method

Cream the butter, add gradually sugar, egg well beaten, and wine; beat while heating over hot water.

# Strawberry Sauce

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- ⅔ cup strawberries
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- White 1 egg

## Method

Cream the butter, add sugar gradually, egg beaten until stiff, and strawberries. Beat until fruit is mashed.

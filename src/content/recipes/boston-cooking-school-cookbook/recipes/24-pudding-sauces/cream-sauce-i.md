# Cream Sauce I

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup thick cream
- ¼ cup milk
- ⅓ cup powdered sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Mix cream and milk, beat until stiff, using egg-beater; add sugar and vanilla.

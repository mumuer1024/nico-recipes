# Molasses Sauce

## Ingredients

- 1 cup molasses
- 1½ tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice or
- 1 tablespoon vinegar

## Method

Boil molasses and butter five minutes; remove from fire and add lemon juice.

# Orange Sauce

## Ingredients

- Whites 3 eggs
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- Juice and rind 2 oranges
- Juice 1 lemon

## Method

Beat whites until stiff, add sugar gradually, and continue beating; add rind and fruit juices.

# Stuffing I

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cracker crumbs
- ⅓ cup butter
- ⅓ cup boiling water
- Salt and Pepper
- Powdered sage, summer savory, or marjoram

## Method

Melt butter in water, and pour over crackers, to which seasonings have been added.

# Duck Stuffing (Peanut)

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup cracker crumbs
- ½ cup shelled peanuts, finely chopped
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Few drops onion juice
- Salt and pepper
- Cayenne

## Method

Mix ingredients in the order given.

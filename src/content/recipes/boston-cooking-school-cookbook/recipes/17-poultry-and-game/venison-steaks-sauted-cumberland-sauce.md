# Venison Steaks, Sautéd, Cumberland Sauce

## Method

Cut venison steaks in circular pieces and use trimmings for the making of stock. Sauté steaks in hot buttered frying-pan and serve with

**Cumberland Sauce.** Soak two tablespoons citron, cut in
julienne-shaped pieces, two tablespoons glacéd cherries, and one
tablespoon Sultana raisins, in Port wine for several hours. Drain and
cook fruit five minutes in one-third cup Port wine. Add one-half tumbler
currant jelly, and, as soon as jelly is dissolved, add one and one-third
cups Brown Sauce, and two tablespoons shredded almonds.

# Oyster Stuffing

## Ingredients

- 3 cups stale bread crumbs
- ½ cup melted butter
- Salt and pepper
- Few drops onion juice
- 1 pint oysters

## Method

Mix ingredients in the order given, add oysters, cleaned and drained from their liquor.

# Turkey Stuffing (Swedish Style)

## Ingredients

- 2 cups stale bread crumbs
- ⅔ cup melted butter
- ½ cup raisins, seeded and cut in pieces
- ½ cup English walnut meats, broken in pieces
- Salt and pepper
- Sage

## Method

Mix ingredients in the order given.

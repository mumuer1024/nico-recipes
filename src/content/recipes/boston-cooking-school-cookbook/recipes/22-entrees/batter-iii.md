# Batter III

## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅔ cup milk
- 1 egg

## Method

Mix and sift dry ingredients, add milk gradually, and egg well beaten.

# Cauliflower Fritters

## Ingredients

- Cold cooked cauliflower
- Batter V
- Salt and pepper

## Method

Sprinkle pieces of cauliflower with salt and pepper and dip in Batter I or V. Fry in deep fat, and drain on brown paper.

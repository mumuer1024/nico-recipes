# Batter I

## Ingredients

- 1 cup bread flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Few grains pepper
- ⅔ cup milk
- 2 eggs

## Method

Mix flour, salt, and pepper. Add milk gradually, and eggs well beaten.

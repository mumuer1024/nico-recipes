# Snow Cake

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup milk
- 1⅔ cups flour
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder
- Whites 2 eggs
- ½ teaspoon vanilla or
- ¼ teaspoon almond extract

## Method

Follow recipe for mixing butter cakes. Bake forty-five minutes in a deep narrow pan.

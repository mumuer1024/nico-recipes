# Fig Éclair

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter (scant)
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup milk
- 1⅞ cups flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- Whites 4 eggs
- ½ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Follow recipe for mixing butter cakes. Bake in shallow pans, put between layers Fig Filling, and sprinkle top with powdered sugar.

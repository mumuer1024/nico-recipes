# Lily Cake

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup milk
- 1¾ cups flour
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder
- Whites 3 eggs
- ⅓ teaspoon lemon extract
- ⅔ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Follow recipe for mixing butter cakes.

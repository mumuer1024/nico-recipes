# Cream Pie I

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 1¾ cups flour
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder

## Method

Mix as One Egg Cake. Bake in round layer cake pans. Put Cream Filling between layers and sprinkle top with powdered sugar.

# Spanish Cake

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- Yolks 2 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 1¾ cups flour
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- Whites 2 eggs

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given. Bake in shallow tins and spread between and on top Caramel Frosting.

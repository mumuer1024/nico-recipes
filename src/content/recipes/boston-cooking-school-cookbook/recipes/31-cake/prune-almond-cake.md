# Prune Almond Cake

## Method

Bake one-half Corn-starch Cake mixture in a dripping-pan. Cut in two crosswise, spread between layers Prune Almond Filling, and cover top with White Mountain Cream.

**Prune Almond Filling.** To one-half the recipe for White Mountain
Cream add eight soft prunes stoned and cut in pieces, and one-fourth cup
almonds blanched and cut in pieces.

# Imperial Cake

## Ingredients

- ½ lb. butter
- ½ lb. sugar
- Yolks 5 eggs
- Whites 5 eggs
- Grated rind ½ lemon
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- ½ lb. raisins, seeded and cut in pieces
- ½ cup walnut meat, broken in pieces
- ½ lb. flour
- ¼ teaspoon soda

## Method

Mix same as Pound Cake, adding raisins dredged with flour, and nuts at the last.

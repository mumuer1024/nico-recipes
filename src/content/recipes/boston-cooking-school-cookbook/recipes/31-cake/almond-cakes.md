# Almond Cakes

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- ¾ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 1⅓ cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup Jordan almonds, blanched and cut in pieces

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given, and bake in individual cake pans.

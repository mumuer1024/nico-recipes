# Meringue I

## Ingredients

- Whites 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
- ½ tablespoon lemon juice or
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Beat whites until stiff, add sugar gradually and continue beating, then add flavoring.

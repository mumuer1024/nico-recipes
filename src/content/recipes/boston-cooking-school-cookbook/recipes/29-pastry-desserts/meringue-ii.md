# Meringue II

## Ingredients

- Whites 3 eggs
- 7½ tablespoons powdered sugar
- ½ teaspoon lemon extract or
- ⅓ teaspoon vanilla

## Method

Beat whites until stiff, add four tablespoons sugar gradually, and beat vigorously; fold in remaining sugar, and add flavoring.

# Tomato Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 can tomatoes
- 1 pint water
- 12 peppercorns
- Bit of bay leaf
- 4 cloves
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon soda
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 1 slice onion

## Method

Cook tomatoes, water, peppercorns, bay leaf, cloves, and sugar twenty minutes; strain, and add salt and soda; bind, and strain into tureen.

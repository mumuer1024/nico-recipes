# Coffee Fondant

## Ingredients

- 2½ lbs. sugar
- 1½ cups cold water
- ¼ cup ground coffee
- ¼ teaspoon cream of tartar

## Method

Put water and coffee in saucepan, and heat to boiling-point. Strain through double cheese-cloth; then add sugar and cream of tartar. Boil, and work same as White Fondant.

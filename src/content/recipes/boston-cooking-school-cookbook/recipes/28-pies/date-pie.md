# Date Pie

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- ⅓ pound sugar dates
- 2 eggs
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Few gratings nutmeg

## Method

Cook dates with milk twenty minutes in top of double boiler. Strain, and rub through sieve, then add eggs and salt. Bake same as Custard Pie.

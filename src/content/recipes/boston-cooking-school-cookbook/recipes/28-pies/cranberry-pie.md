# Cranberry Pie

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups cranberries
- ½ cup water
- ¾ cup sugar

## Method

Put ingredients in saucepan in order given, and cook ten minutes; cool, and bake in one crust, with a rim, and strips across the top.

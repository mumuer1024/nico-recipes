# Imperial Cookies

## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 2½ cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon lemon extract
- ½ teaspoon grated nutmeg

## Method

Mix and bake same as Vanilla Wafers.

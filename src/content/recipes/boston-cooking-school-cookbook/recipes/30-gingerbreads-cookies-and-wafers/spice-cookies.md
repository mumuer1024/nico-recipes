# Spice Cookies

## Ingredients

- ½ cup molasses
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1½ tablespoons butter
- 1½ tablespoons lard
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 2 cups flour
- ½ teaspoon soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon clove
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg

## Method

Heat molasses to boiling-point. Add sugar, shortening, and milk. Mix and sift dry ingredients, and add to first mixture. Chill thoroughly, and proceed as with Ginger Snaps.

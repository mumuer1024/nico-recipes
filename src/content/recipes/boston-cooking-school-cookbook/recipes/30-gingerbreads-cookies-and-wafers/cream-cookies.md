# Cream Cookies

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup thin cream
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons yellow ginger
- Flour to roll

## Method

Mix and bake same as Vanilla Wafers.

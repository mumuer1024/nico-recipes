# Cream Dressing I

## Ingredients

- ½ tablespoon salt
- ½ tablespoon mustard
- ¾ tablespoon sugar
- 1 egg slightly beaten
- 2½ tablespoons melted butter
- ¾ cup cream
- ¼ cup vinegar

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given, adding vinegar very slowly. Cook over boiling water, stirring constantly until mixture thickens, strain and cool.

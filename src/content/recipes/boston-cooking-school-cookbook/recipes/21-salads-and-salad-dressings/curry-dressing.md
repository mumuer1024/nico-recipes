# Curry Dressing

## Ingredients

- ¾ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon curry powder
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 5 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons vinegar

## Method

Mix ingredients in order given and stir until well blended.

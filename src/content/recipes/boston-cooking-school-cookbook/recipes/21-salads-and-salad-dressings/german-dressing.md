# German Dressing

## Ingredients

- ½ cup thick cream
- 3 tablespoons vinegar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Few grains pepper

## Method

Beat cream until stiff, using Dover Egg-beater. Add salt, pepper, and vinegar very slowly, continuing the beating.

# Club French Dressing

## Ingredients

- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 2 tablespoons brandy
- 2 tablespoons Tarragon vinegar
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Method

Mix ingredients and stir until well blended.

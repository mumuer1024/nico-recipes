# Sauce Finiste

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons butter
- ½ teaspoon mustard
- Few grains cayenne
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1½ teaspoons Worcestershire Sauce
- ¾ cup stewed and strained tomatoes

## Method

Cook butter until well browned, and add remaining ingredients.

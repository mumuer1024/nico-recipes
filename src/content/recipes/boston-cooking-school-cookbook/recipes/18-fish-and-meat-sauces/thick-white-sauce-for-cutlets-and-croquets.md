# Thick White Sauce (for Cutlets and Croquets)

## Ingredients

- 2½ tablespoons butter
- ¼ cup corn-starch or
- ⅓ cup flour
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Few grains pepper

## Method

Make same as Thin White Sauce.

# Maître d'Hôtel Butter

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- ½ tablespoon finely chopped parsley
- ¾ tablespoon lemon juice

## Method

Put butter in a bowl, and with small wooden spoon work until creamy. Add salt, pepper, and parsley, then lemon juice very slowly.

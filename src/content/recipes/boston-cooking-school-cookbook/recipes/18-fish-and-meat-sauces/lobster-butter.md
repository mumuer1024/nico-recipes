# Lobster Butter

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- Lobster coral

## Method

Clean, wipe, and force coral through a fine sieve. Put in a mortar with butter, and pound until well blended. This butter is used in Lobster Soup and Sauces to give color and richness.

# Horseradish Sauce II

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons cracker crumbs
- ⅓ cup grated horseradish root
- 1½ cups milk
- 3 tablespoons butter
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper

## Method

Cook first three ingredients twenty minutes in double boiler. Add butter, salt, and pepper.

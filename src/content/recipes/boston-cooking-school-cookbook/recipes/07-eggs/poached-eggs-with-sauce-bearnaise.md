# Poached Eggs with Sauce Bearnaise

## Method

Poach six eggs, arrange in serving dish, cover eggs alternately with red and yellow sauce, and garnish with parsley.

**Sauce Bearnaise.** Beat yolks three eggs slightly, add three
tablespoons olive oil, two tablespoons hot water, three-fourths
tablespoon tarragon vinegar, one-fourth teaspoon salt, and a few grains
cayenne. Cook over boiling water until mixture thickens. Color one-half
the sauce with tomato purée (tomatoes drained from their liquor, stewed,
strained, and cooked until reduced to a thick pulp).

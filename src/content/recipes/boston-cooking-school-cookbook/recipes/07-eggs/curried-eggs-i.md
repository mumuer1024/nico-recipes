# Curried Eggs I

## Ingredients

- 3 "hard-boiled" eggs
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon curry powder
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup hot milk

## Method

Melt butter, add flour and seasonings, and gradually hot milk. Cut eggs in eighths lengthwise, and reheat in sauce.

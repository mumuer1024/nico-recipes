# Bread Omelet

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup stale bread crumbs
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 1 tablespoon butter

## Method

Soak bread crumbs fifteen minutes in milk, add beaten yolks and seasonings, fold in whites. Cook and serve as Plain Omelet.

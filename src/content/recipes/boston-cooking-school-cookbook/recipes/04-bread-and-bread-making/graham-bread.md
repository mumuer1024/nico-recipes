# Graham Bread

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups hot liquid (water, or milk and water)
- ⅓ cup molasses
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ¼ yeast cake dissolved in
- ¼ cup lukewarm water
- 3 cups flour
- 3 cups Graham flour

## Method

Prepare and bake as Entire Wheat Bread. The bran remaining in sieve after sifting Graham flour should be discarded.

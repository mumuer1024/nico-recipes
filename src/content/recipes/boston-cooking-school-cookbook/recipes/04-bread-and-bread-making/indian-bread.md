# Indian Bread

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups Graham flour
- 1 cup Indian meal
- ½ tablespoon soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup molasses
- 1⅔ cups milk

## Method

Mix and steam same as Boston Brown Bread.

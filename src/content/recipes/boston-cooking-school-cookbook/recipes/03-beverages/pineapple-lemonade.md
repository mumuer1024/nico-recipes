# Pineapple Lemonade

## Ingredients

- 1 pint water
- 1 quart ice-water
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 can grated pineapple
- Juice 3 lemons

## Method

Make syrup by boiling water and sugar ten minutes; add pineapple and lemon juice, cool, strain, and add ice-water.

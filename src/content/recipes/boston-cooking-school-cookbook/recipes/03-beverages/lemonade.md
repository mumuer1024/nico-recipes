# Lemonade

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- 1 pint water

## Method

Make syrup by boiling sugar and water twelve minutes; add fruit juice, cool, and dilute with ice-water to suit individual tastes. Lemon syrup may be bottled and kept on hand to use as needed.

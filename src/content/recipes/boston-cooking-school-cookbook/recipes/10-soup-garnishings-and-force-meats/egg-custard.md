# Egg Custard

## Ingredients

- Yolks 2 eggs
- Few grains salt
- 2 tablespoons milk

## Method

Beat eggs slightly, add milk and salt. Pour into small buttered cup, place in pan of hot water, and bake until firm; cool, remove from cup, and cut in fancy shapes with French vegetable cutters.

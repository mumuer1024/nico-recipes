# Fritter Beans

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons milk
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup flour

## Method

Beat egg until light, add milk, salt, and flour. Put through colander or pastry tube into deep fat, and fry until brown; drain on brown paper.

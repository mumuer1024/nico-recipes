# Royal Custard

## Ingredients

- Yolks 3 eggs
- 1 egg
- ½ cup Consommé
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- Slight grating nutmeg
- Few grains cayenne

## Method

Beat eggs slightly, add Consommé and seasonings. Pour into a small buttered tin mould, place in pan of hot water, and bake until firm; cool, remove from mould, and cut in fancy shapes.

# Minced Mutton

## Ingredients

- 2 cups chopped cooked mutton
- Yolks 6 "hard-boiled" eggs
- ¾ teaspoon mixed mustard
- Salt
- Cayenne
- 1 cup of cream
- ¼ cup wine

## Method

Mash the yolks, and season with mustard, salt, and cayenne. Add cream and mutton. When thoroughly heated add wine. Serve on toast.

# Scotch Woodcock

## Ingredients

- 4 "hard-boiled" eggs
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1½ tablespoons flour
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Few grains cayenne
- Anchovy essence

## Method

Make a thin white sauce of butter, flour, milk, and seasonings; add eggs finely chopped, and season with Anchovy essence. Serve same as Welsh Rarebit I.

# Macaroni with White Sauce

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup macaroni broken in inch pieces
- 2 quarts boiling water
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1½ cups White Sauce

## Method

Cook as for Boiled Macaroni, and reheat in White Sauce.

**White Sauce.** Melt two tablespoons butter, add two tablespoons
flour with one-half teaspoon salt, and pour on slowly one and
one-half cups scalded milk.
